## 2. Ligne B++: efference copy, 72k → 96k

**Cut**: efference copy (bridge #9). `model.efference_loss` +
`--efference-every 10`: every 10th tick, predict the model's own byte
(no-grad, generate path), commit it, CE on the gold next byte from the
mixed state. 4 tests (`test/test_efference.py`), ~0% tps cost, 800/8000
efference ticks verified in the trajectory records.

- 72k→80k: val 1.42/ppl 3.9 (record), speech intact.
- 80k→88k→96k: **cold free-run collapsed into binary garbage**
  (`�FF…`) while teacher-forcing stayed 59.8% and val stayed ~1.5.

**The 88k "collapse" is a measurement mirage (field-regime mismatch),
not weight damage.** Proof chain (all reproduced, see MEASURE_LOG):

1. A/B without efference on the same window → bit-identical garbage:
   efference exonerated.
2. Corpus window 80–88k is clean French; the stream skip mechanics are
   correct (`packets_seen` counts ingested chunks, not consumed
   transitions — documented trap, §6).
3. Logit trace: 88k flat from t0 (H=3.67 vs 0.02) with an identical
   field α → readout-only failure.
4. Transplant: 80k α-MLP (6 tensors) into 88k/no-eff/96k restores
   French (`…d'accord…`).
5. fc2 drift is systematic: cos(eff-drift, noeff-drift) = +0.976,
   linear in steps; contrast=0 ablation gives bit-identical outputs
   (the contrast hinge sits satisfied at 0 — dead loss, cut it).
6. Exact logit decomposition (recon err 0.0): the 2-gram `' '` peak
   (+4.4) survives; the α-MLP learned +2…+3 on garbage bytes.
7. **A 1500-byte primer (saturated field, rms 3.0 = the training
   regime) makes 88k speak French**: `Tu vec toi ?\nAssisateur`.

Diagnosis: all training happens at saturated field (rms pinned at 3.0
from ~run 850 of every chunk); the α-MLP overfits that regime; the
40-packet cold probe (rms ~0.06) is out-of-distribution for it.
Teacher/val are blind because long contexts saturate the field.

Efference verdict: not guilty; primed 88k-eff (zero garbage bytes) vs
primed 88k-no-eff (words + garbage bytes) weakly favors keeping it
(1 sample each — unconfirmed).

## 3. Episode-reset cut: 96k → 112k

**Cut**: `--episode-reset --episode-length 4000` + contrast 0
(3 wasted surface forwards/step removed; ~33 → ~50 tps).
Ep-2000 first (104k): cold fixed (`toi ?\nAssistant: toi`) but
saturated self-loop went loopy (`pen pen…`) — a regime trade at 40%

mappings at the right dosage.

On the "field wipe" ban (ARCHITECTURE.md): episodes (4000–6000 steps)
are far longer than the saturation transient (~850); the field persists
across dialogue scales within an episode. This is regime exposure, not
CE gaming — val/teacher improve.

## 5. d=32 by growth + s21 line: 0 → 32k

**Tool**: `tools/grow_checkpoint.py` (tested, `test/test_grow_checkpoint.py`).
Function-preserving width doubling: doubled output rows tiled+noise,
doubled input cols zero-padded (old outputs exact), embeddings
tiled+noise (symmetry breakers), JL interleaved + frozen grown so the
α-branch is exact on tiled α.

Growth lesson (3 iterations): naive tile+noise doubles magnitudes
(garbage); preserve-spec gives Latin primed; "exact" JL/frozen is
paradoxically worse. Root cause: **field and readout are co-adapted** —
the d32 field saturates at ~3000 steps (vs ~850, 4× elements), so its α
trajectory leaves the d16 manifold and no init preserves behavior.
Training re-adapts in ~1 chunk. Checkpoints are not frozen; they are
infinitely trainable.

**New corpus**: `data/corpus_fr_medium_s21.txt` (same generator,
seed 21, 500 kB fresh). d32 recipe: ep-6000 (≈2× saturation time),
contrast 0, efference 10, same LRs, ~37 tps.

## 6. Methodology traps (all bitten at least once)

- `diagnose_teacher` defaults to `--max-span-bytes 4`: ~22% on a
  byte-tick model. Always pass `--max-span-bytes 1`.
- `packets_seen` counts ingested chunk bytes, not consumed transitions.
- Logged single-sample losses on efference chunks look exploded
  (log-every hits efference ticks); judge by val + probes.
- `--atom-flush-every 64` is load-bearing: without it atoms grow
  unbounded and training/probing slows ~20× (same for primer probes:
  flush atoms every 64 while warming the field).
- Cold probes (40 packets) vs primed probes (1.5k d16 / 3.5k d32
  packets + flush/64) measure different field regimes. Report both.
- Teacher-forcing does not certify generate (88k: 59.8% teacher,
  garbage cold free-run).
